Strategic outcomes
The four developments that defined Month 8
  • Visibility & content performance. 749 impressions and 21 likes across Posts 29–32, with interaction from senior supply-chain, cybersecurity and technology leaders.
  • Network penetration. 1,217 unique targeted connection requests sent to senior professionals across 142 companies, yielding 402 confirmed connections — a 33% conversion rate.
  • Seniority quality. Of the 1,236 request records, 953 (77.1%) sit at Director level or above — an exceptionally senior outreach universe, with 68.4% of records mapped to the 46 named target companies.
  • Market mapping. Outreach concentrated in the Big Four, major IT services and telecom leaders — led by Accenture (87 targeted, 40 confirmed), EY (70 targeted, 39 confirmed) and Deloitte (51 targeted, 37 confirmed).
